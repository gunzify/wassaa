## PART A — WHAT THIS MEANS, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

1. **Not every profile on WASSAA is a real person.** Some profiles are operated by WASSAA. We call these **Automated Profiles**.
2. **Automated Profiles are marked in the app.** Their profile card and their conversation carry an **"Automated profile"** badge (also described on screen as *"AI persona — replies are generated, not written by a person"*).
3. **Replies from an Automated Profile are generated**, either from pre-written scripts or by an artificial-intelligence system. The person in the photograph does not exist, or has licensed the image; the words are produced by software.
4. **Replies may arrive after a delay.** That delay is deliberate scheduling by our software. It is **not** evidence that a human being was typing, reading, or thinking about your message.
5. **Chatting uses Credits.** Credits spent on messages and actions are spent whether the other side is a person or an Automated Profile. Because Automated Profiles are marked, you can always tell before you spend.
6. **You can avoid them entirely.** In **Settings → Discovery → "Show automated profiles"** you may switch them off. Conversations already open remain readable; no further automated replies are generated for you.
7. **You cannot buy an Automated Profile's attention.** Automated Profiles never solicit money, do not send payment requests, and will never ask you to move to another app or platform.

If anything in Part A is inaccurate about our product at the time you read it, the product is wrong and we will fix it. The legal terms below are the binding version.

## PART B — FORMAL DISCLOSURE

### 1. Definitions

1.1 Capitalised terms not defined in this Disclosure have the meanings given in the Terms of Service.

1.2 "**Automated Profile**" means a profile on the Service that is not operated by a natural person, including any persona, character, or synthetic identity operated by us or on our behalf, together with any messages, reactions, or Content generated by scripted logic, templates, or artificial-intelligence systems.

1.3 "**Generated Content**" means any Content in or from an Automated Profile that is produced by software, including pre-authored scripts, variable substitution, intent-classified responses, and output from a language model.

1.4 "**Marker**" means the visual indicator applied to an Automated Profile in its profile view and in its conversation view.

### 2. Identification

2.1 Every Automated Profile carries a Marker. The Marker is displayed: (a) on the profile card; (b) in the conversation header; and (c) adjacent to the first generated message in the conversation.

2.2 The Marker is applied by the Service and is not removable by the Member. Members shall not remove, obscure, or replicate a Marker, and shall not represent an Automated Profile as a natural person or a natural person as an Automated Profile.

2.3 Members may filter Automated Profiles from discovery. The filter is provided at no charge and does not require a paid tier.

### 3. Nature and Origin of Generated Content

3.1 Generated Content may be authored in advance, selected from a corpus according to a classification of your message, or produced at the time of interaction by a third-party language model.

3.2 Generated Content is **not** attributable to any natural person, does not represent the views or knowledge of any natural person, and may be inaccurate, incomplete, repetitive, or inappropriate. It must not be relied upon as advice of any kind, including medical, legal, financial, safety, or professional advice.

3.3 Photographs and media associated with Automated Profiles are synthetic or licensed. They may depict persons who do not exist. Any resemblance to a real individual is coincidental.

3.4 Timing. Generated Content may be transmitted after a scheduled delay intended to approximate conversational rhythm. Such timing is a product-design decision and is not a representation that a natural person is present, attentive, or responding.

3.5 Automated Profiles do not consume, read, or reply to Content outside the conversation to which they are party, save as described in the Privacy Policy.

### 4. Charges and Credits

4.1 Where a Member sends a message, performs an action, or accesses a feature that consumes Credits, Credits are consumed at the disclosed rate irrespective of whether the counterparty is a natural person or an Automated Profile.

4.2 **Because Automated Profiles are marked before the point of interaction, a Member is at all times able to identify the nature of the counterparty prior to incurring any charge.**

4.3 We do not operate any feature in which the identity of an Automated Profile is withheld, disguised, or made discoverable only after payment.

4.4 Credits consumed in an Automated Profile conversation are subject to the refund provisions of the Terms.

### 5. Limits We Observe

5.1 We do not use Automated Profiles to: (a) solicit money, gifts, or payments from Members; (b) induce a Member to purchase Credits by representing that a specific natural person is interested in them; (c) move a Member to a third-party platform; or (d) obtain identity documents, financial credentials, or one-time passcodes.

5.2 Automated Profiles do not claim to be a specific, identifiable real individual, and do not claim to hold a rank, office, or appointment.

5.3 Automated Profiles are subject to the same content standards as Member Content, and can be reported through the in-app reporting flow.
