## 3. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

### 3.1 Data you provide

(a) **Account and authentication data:** mobile telephone number, electronic-mail address, one-time passcodes and their delivery metadata, Google identifier and profile data where you elect Google sign-in, session and refresh tokens, account identifiers.

(b) **Profile data:** name or display name, date of birth, gender, pronouns, city and state, profession category and title, employer or organisation, service branch, rank, duty station (military and public-service fields are optional and Member-supplied), bio, interests, dating intent, lifestyle and values answers, match preferences, and profile photographs and other media.

(c) **Identity-review data (special category):** where you elect identity review, the document type, a document reference number (stored in masked form), a photograph of the identity document, and a live-captured selfie. Identity documents and selfies may reveal nationality, sex, date of birth, and — depending on the document — other sensitive attributes. We process this material solely for identity review and trust and safety.

(d) **Communications and Content:** messages (including text, captions and media), reactions, reports and their narratives, appeals, support correspondence, and any Content you submit in prompts or answers.

(e) **Transaction data:** credit balances and ledger entries, purchases, plan and consumable identifiers, amounts, currency, payment status, processor references, and refund or chargeback records. We do not receive or store full payment-card numbers; card details are submitted directly to our payment processor.

(f) **Verification and moderation records:** reviewer decisions, reasons, timestamps, administrator identity, and audit-log entries relating to your Account.

### 3.2 Data collected automatically

(a) **Device and technical data:** IP address, device type, operating-system and browser version, language, time zone, application version, and identifiers necessary for session management.

(b) **Usage data:** screens and features accessed, actions taken (swipes, likes, passes, matches, messages, purchases), timestamps, and error and performance diagnostics.

(c) **Approximate location:** the city and state you select, and — where you enable distance-based discovery — device-derived coordinates used to compute a distance band. Coordinates are used to compute a band; the Service does not display raw coordinates of any Member to any other Member.

(d) **Rate-limiting and abuse data:** per-account request counters, device and network signals used to detect automated abuse, and related security logs.

(e) **Local storage:** authentication tokens, session identifiers, preferences and interface state, stored in your browser or application storage (see Clause 12).

### 3.3 Data from third parties

Identity and authentication providers; our payment processor (transaction status and references); our communications providers (delivery status of one-time passcodes); our hosting, storage, and infrastructure providers (logs and diagnostics); and third-party language-model providers where Automated Profiles with generated replies are enabled.

### 3.4 Data we do not seek

We do not seek to collect data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade-union membership, genetic or biometric identifiers for identification purposes, health data, or data concerning sex life or sexual orientation — save where identity-review material incidentally reveals such attributes, or where you volunteer such information in free-text fields. Please do not volunteer such information in your bio or messages.

## 5. AUTOMATED PROCESSING AND AUTOMATED PROFILES

5.1 **Ranking and discovery.** Discovery results are ordered by automated logic that takes account of your stated preferences, location band, profile completeness, verification status, activity and — for paid tiers — placement entitlements. This is profiling; it does not produce legal effects and does not significantly affect you in a comparable manner, but you may object as described in Clause 10.

5.2 **Automated Profiles.** Some profiles are not natural persons and some messages are generated by software or artificial-intelligence systems. Those profiles are marked in the interface, and you can filter them. The full description is in the Synthetic Profiles and Automated Messaging Disclosure.

5.3 **Generated replies.** Where a third-party language-model provider is engaged, the conversation history and the profile fields necessary to produce a reply are transmitted to that provider for the purpose of generating that reply. We do not authorise providers to use that data to train their general models where we are able to contract otherwise, and we do not sell it.

5.4 **Safety automation.** Automated systems may flag Content or behaviour for review, may apply rate limits, and may temporarily restrict features. Decisions that materially and adversely affect your Account are subject to human review on request.

5.5 **No solely automated decisions with legal effect.** We do not make decisions producing legal effects, or similarly significant effects, concerning you on a solely automated basis. You may request human intervention, express your point of view, and contest a decision by contacting **[PRIVACY EMAIL]**.
